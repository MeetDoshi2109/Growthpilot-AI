## ✨ The idea in one sentence

> **GrowthPilot doesn't just tell a merchant what happened — it identifies what should happen next, gets the right approval, executes the action, verifies the result, and measures the impact.**

---

## 🎯 Built for Track 1 — Merchant Growth AI

> **Build the AI business partner for every Paytm merchant.**

GrowthPilot AI is focused **exclusively on Track 1**.

The product helps merchants understand their business, discover revenue opportunities, decide what action to take, execute approved growth workflows, and measure the outcome.

### 🚀 Track 1 Capabilities

- 📈 Revenue intelligence
- 👥 Customer intelligence
- 🎯 AI growth opportunities
- 🔁 Customer win-back campaigns
- 🛍️ Cross-sell & upsell opportunities
- 📦 Inventory intelligence
- 💳 Payment-recovery opportunities
- 📊 Sales forecasting
- 🧠 Explainable recommendations
- ⚙️ Autonomous growth workflows
- ✅ Action verification
- 📜 Merchant activity & AI audit trail

**Track 2 and Track 3 are not separate product tracks in this project.**  
Agent orchestration, controlled execution, approvals, and verification are used **inside the Track 1 merchant-growth experience** to make the AI business partner actually get growth work done.

# 🛡️ Trust & Safety by Design

GrowthPilot follows a critical principle:

> **The LLM proposes. Controlled systems decide and execute.**

### The LLM cannot directly mutate financial state.

Every action follows:

```text
Validate
   ↓
Policy Check
   ↓
Approval (when required)
   ↓
Execute
   ↓
Verify
   ↓
Audit
```

### Risk levels

```text
L0 ─ Read-only
     ↓
L1 ─ Recommendation
     ↓
L2 ─ Low-risk action
     ↓
L3 ─ Merchant approval required
     ↓
L4 ─ Sensitive financial action
```

Additional safeguards include:

- 🔐 Tenant isolation
- 🧾 Audit logging
- 🔗 Hash-chained audit records
- 🛑 Kill switch
- 🎚️ Autonomy control
- 🕵️ PII masking in AI/audit contexts
- 🔢 Numeric grounding
- 🔁 Idempotent actions
- ✅ Post-action verification

# 🧭 Design Principles

### 1. AI must be useful

Every AI output should lead toward a business decision or action.

### 2. Numbers must be grounded

Business metrics come from data.

### 3. Actions must be controlled

AI does not directly mutate sensitive state.

### 4. Automation must be observable

Every action has a lifecycle and audit record.

### 5. Financial UX must be explainable

Never hide the "why".

### 6. Simulation must be honest

Demo behavior is always labeled as simulated.

### 7. The merchant stays in control

Autonomy is earned through explicit policies and approval gates.
